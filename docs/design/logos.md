# Logos

The customer's files, in `web/public/brand/` (504 × 142 px PNG).

- `streamline-white.png` (repo: `streamline-fs-white.png`): single-ink white. Only on `chrome` or `mark-blue`: the navigation rail at 30px height, the login brand panel at 40px.
- `streamline-colour.png` (repo: `streamline-fs.png`): cobalt wordmark, red stripe, black "FLIGHT SUPPORT". Only on pure white: exports, printed documents, the client portal top bar.

No SVG of the mark exists in the repository; request vector files from the customer before producing print material larger than A4. Never redraw, recolour or crop either file.
