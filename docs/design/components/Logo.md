# Logo

The customer's Streamline Flight Support logo, used as supplied, white on `chrome` and colour on white.

Use `streamline-fs-white.png` in the rail (30px high, followed by a `chrome-line` divider and `SOC` in `overline` weight) and on the login brand panel (40px high). Use `streamline-fs.png` only on a white ground: PDF and XLSX exports, the client portal top bar, printed documents.

The consumer provides nothing but the placement; the files live in `web/public/brand/`.

Do not recolour, outline, crop, add shadows, or put the colour version on `canvas`, a tint or a dark ground. Do not draw a substitute mark. In the collapsed rail (64px) show `SOC` in Geologica 600 13px instead of shrinking the logo.
